Method

Fix the process, then write the code.

I think in systems. I treat a process as a black box, test it, watch it under load, and the bottleneck shows itself. Fixing the process comes before writing code; cost is a design input; every solution assumes it will be audited.

01

Observe

Shadow the people doing the work, on the floor, on a real day.

02

Map

Write down every handoff, wait and workaround as it actually happens.

03

Find the bottleneck

Load the process and watch where it stalls. There is usually one.

04

Redesign

Change the sequence and the ownership before touching a tool.

05

Build thin in sprints

Ship the smallest working slice to real users, then widen it.

06

Secure & govern

Roles, verified handoffs and an audit trail from the first release.

07

Measure

Compare days, hours and complaints before and after. Keep what moved.
